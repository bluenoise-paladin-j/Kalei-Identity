If you want to change…

  1. Butterflies too big or too small →
    CFG.wingPlane js/config.js:506
    Not sizeMin or colSizeMin — those set size, the unit the pick radius, the wingbeat bob and tagBodyDrop are all quoted in.
  2. Flight too busy or too still →
    CFG.wander js/config.js:37
    Scales all three noise amplitudes live. The amplitudes themselves live in keyboard.js spawn and have been cut twice.
  3. Small wings still too small →
    CFG.wingFitSeam js/config.js:514
    The seam recentre is the part that lifts the small ones. wingFitFill takes more headroom overall.
  4. Too many butterflies at once →
    CFG.maxCollected js/config.js:307
    Ceiling around 36: Wings.MAX_UNIQUE is 64 and the 26 keys hold 26 slots for good.
